Emergency Water Damage Restoration Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Pipes running through unheated areas need insulation to keep them from freezing and bursting.
  • Replace old supply hoses on washers, toilets and faucets before they burst.
  • Being able to find the electrical panel quickly means wet areas can be de-energized without risk.
  • A sump pump should be tried out before storms arrive, ideally with a backup battery installed.
  • Before anything goes wrong, store the phone number for an emergency restoration crew.
  • Important paperwork and valuables belong above floor level, most of all on the lowest story.

When to Call for Emergency Water Damage Restoration in Taylor

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Sewage has backed up, or the water is dark with a foul odor.
  • Trapped water overhead has made part of the ceiling bulge or sag.
  • Standing water is spreading across floors toward other rooms or down the stairs.
  • An outlet, a light fixture or the ceiling above has water dripping out of it.
  • Water has reached the electrical panel, appliances or power strips on the floor.
  • Walls on the lower floor are wet from top to bottom because of a leak overhead.
  • You have no way to locate or get to the shutoff while water continues to pour.
