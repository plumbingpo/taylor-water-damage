Sewage Backup Causes and Prevention in Taylor, MI

Home / Blog / Sewage Backup Causes and Prevention in Taylor, MI

For a homeowner, a backed-up sewer line ranks near the top of the list of unpleasant emergencies. Raw sewage entering your living spaces creates immediate health hazards, causes extensive property damage, and requires professional cleanup that can cost thousands of dollars. Learn the reasons these backups happen, act on a few preventive measures, and you cut the odds considerably. Taylor Restoration wrote this guide to walk Taylor, MI owners through the usual causes and some practical ways to keep a property protected.

Why Sewage Backups Happen

A backup happens when wastewater leaving your property cannot reach the septic tank or the municipal sewer system the way it should. Usual culprits are tree roots growing into sewer pipes; lines clogged by a buildup of hair, grease, and debris; pipes that are old or have collapsed; combined sewer overflow events; an overloaded municipal system in heavy rain; and toilets used to flush the wrong things, such as feminine products, wipes, and too much paper.

How Tree Roots Block Sewer Pipes

Tree roots are one of the most common causes of sewer line blockages in Taylor. A sewer pipe is a steady supply of moisture, which is exactly what roots go looking for. Roots can infiltrate pipes through joints, cracks, and connection points, gradually filling the pipe interior and causing blockages that lead to backups. Older clay tile sewer pipes are particularly vulnerable to root intrusion. Schedule a sewer camera inspection every 2 to 3 years so root trouble is found ahead of any backup.

How to Prevent a Sewage Backup

Protecting your Taylor home from sewage backups involves several preventive measures: install a backwater valve on your sewer connection to prevent municipal system backflows. Never pour grease or cooking oil down drains. Toilet paper is the only thing that belongs in the toilet, so no wipes, no feminine products, and nothing else. Routine inspection and cleaning of your sewer line should be on the calendar. A drain that is running slow needs prompt attention, or it may block completely. Find out where your sewer cleanout is so it can be reached in an emergency. Consider sewer line insurance or a sewage backup endorsement on your homeowner's policy.

Sewage Came Up? Do This First

If you experience a sewage backup, do not attempt cleanup yourself. Serious illness can come from the dangerous pathogens in sewage, among them parasites, bacteria, and viruses.
