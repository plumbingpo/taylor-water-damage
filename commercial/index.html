Common Area Protection

Water Damage Services for HOAs and Managed Communities

A fast and well-coordinated response matters even more when the property is run by a homeowner association or community manager. The risk runs higher in these communities because of older infrastructure, buildings with many units, plumbing that is shared, and common spaces. The HOA programs at Taylor Restoration cover plumbing inspections aimed at prevention, common-area moisture monitoring, emergency coordination with boards and property managers, mold remediation in each affected unit, fast extraction, and structural drying.

Liability management is a critical concern for HOA boards and property managers. When water moves from one unit to the next or reaches a common area, the legal and financial fallout can be significant. We look for weak points before they turn into incidents, and the inspection reports and service history we keep show the association did its due diligence. After every visit you receive a detailed report, and once a year a property assessment helps the board plan its budget for maintenance and prevention.

Prevention and response plans are developed together with your board, property manager, and maintenance committee so they protect the community and stay within budget. Because people are living on site, crews are trained to keep residents informed, limit the disruption, and get affected areas restored without delay.

Damage Prevention

Commercial Water Damage Risk Assessment and Prevention

The law expects owners of commercial buildings to keep their properties in safe condition, known as a duty of care. Injuries, lawsuits, and damaged property can all trace back to a sudden failure from inadequate waterproofing, concealed moisture, or plumbing past its prime. Taylor Restoration provides professional water damage risk assessments using moisture monitoring equipment and thermal imaging technology that evaluate every area's vulnerability to water intrusion. Findings are delivered in writing, with risks identified, priorities assigned, and specific prevention measures recommended.

These assessments serve a dual purpose: they protect people and property by identifying vulnerabilities, and they demonstrate due diligence that can protect you legally if an incident occurs. Courts and insurance companies look favorably on property owners who maintain regular professional inspections and act on expert recommendations. We recommend annual moisture assessments for all commercial properties, with more frequent inspections for high-risk areas such as basements, mechanical rooms, and aging plumbing systems.
