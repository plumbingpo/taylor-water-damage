Few water damage events are as common, or as potentially devastating to a property, as a pipe that bursts. At 4 to 8 gallons a minute, just one burst can send 240 to 480 gallons each hour into your ceilings, walls and flooring. Pipes burst from freezing, old plumbing, too much water pressure, corrosion or a physical hit, and in every case a fast emergency response is what keeps water from ruining flooring, structural materials, building systems and your belongings.

Day or night, Taylor Restoration responds to burst pipe emergencies across Taylor and the nearby communities. We can help find the shutoff and stop the water, and our team works with licensed plumbers to see that the pipe itself is repaired correctly. As soon as the flow is shut down, extraction starts: portable and truck-mounted units pull standing water out of every affected area as fast as we can.

After the water is out, we dry every material that took on moisture: finished floors, subflooring, wood framing, insulation and drywall. Gravity carries the water through walls, floors and ceilings, so damage from one burst commonly shows up in several rooms and on different floors. Our thermal imaging and moisture detection equipment traces water migration paths so every affected area is identified and properly dried, which prevents the mold growth and structural deterioration that hidden moisture causes.

How to Prevent Burst Pipe Repair Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Before winter, drain outside spigots and take the hoses off them
  • Aging galvanized or polybutylene plumbing should be swapped out ahead of an unexpected failure
  • Monitor your water pressure, because excessive pressure (above 80 PSI) strains pipes and fittings
  • When it freezes, let taps drip and open the cabinets under sinks to let warm air reach the pipes
  • Leave the heat on when you travel in winter so indoor pipes stay above freezing
  • Everyone in the house should be able to locate and turn the main water valve in a hurry

Warning Signs for Burst Pipe Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Water pressure that falls off abruptly at every fixture
  • Hissing or rushing noises in the walls with every tap turned off
  • Rusty or discolored water coming from your taps
  • A hidden leak often shows up first as a much larger water bill
  • A fixture that stops flowing when it is below freezing outside
  • Stale or earthy smells coming from a cabinet or wall cavity
  • Unexplained standing water along baseboards or in the middle of a room
