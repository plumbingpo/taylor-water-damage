Storm Damage Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Have your roof checked annually and fix vulnerable areas ahead of the stormy months
  • Make sure the ground around the house slopes away and downspouts discharge well clear of it
  • Make sure the attic is properly ventilated and insulated to prevent ice dams
  • For insurance purposes, keep a yearly set of pictures showing the state of your property
  • Know how to shut off your main water supply in case a storm damages a pipe
  • Homes in hurricane country benefit from shutters or impact-rated window glass

Warning Signs for Storm Damage Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Missing, damaged or lifted shingles you can see from the ground
  • Damaged or displaced flashing around roof penetrations, valleys or edges
  • Bubbling paint and soft, drooping spots overhead once the rain stops
  • Attic or wall insulation that is damp or matted down following a storm
  • Puddles near skylights, beside a chimney or on the attic floor
  • Damp, earthy odors that appear a few days after the storm
  • Water getting past doors, windows and wall penetrations when wind pushes the rain

Why Storm Damage Repair Is Not a DIY Job

Because one kind of storm damage feeds another, the work needs restoration professionals with real experience. What seems like a small leak indoors can hide widespread water damage in wall cavities, roof decking and insulation, which turns into mold and structural decay unless it is identified and dried properly. Our technicians trace where the water traveled using thermal imaging plus moisture meters, so no affected area is overlooked.

Emergency board-up and tarping also take proper technique to be effective. Tarps put on the wrong way may funnel water into the building or tear loose in the wind. Experienced in storm mitigation, our team picks appropriate methods and materials to keep the building protected until permanent repair work can be scheduled.
